# Evidence photos: diaspora, Europe, Zionism and the Mandate (Parts 3 and 4)

Real materials for the famous documents, places and people between the Middle Ages and 1917. Same rules as `evidence-ancient.md`: 2.5 seconds at most, slow zoom, small label, the narrator points with his staff, and straight back to the drawn story.

**Licences: not verified.** Commons file pages did not open from my workspace, so I have read no licence. Open each file page and read the Licensing box before you download.
- Safe: Public domain, CC0, or CC BY (credit the author).
- Careful: CC BY-SA (share-alike), and anything marked "All rights reserved" or with a museum or archive copyright credit.
- Photos of flat old documents, prints and paintings are usually public domain. A photo taken today of a place (the Venice Ghetto) belongs to its photographer, so the licence matters.
- Photos by the Central Zionist Archives or the Government Press Office may carry their own terms even when uploaded to Commons. Check them.
- Send me files named by item (`alhambra.jpg`, `herzl.jpg`, …) and I'll log each licence in `footage.md`.
