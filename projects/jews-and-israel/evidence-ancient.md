# Evidence photos: the ancient part (Parts 1 and 2)

Real materials for the famous objects and places. Each is shown as a photo on the stage for 2.5 seconds at most, with a slow zoom, a small label, and the narrator pointing at it. Rules are in the `real-footage-editing` skill.

**Licences: not verified.** Wikimedia Commons pages did not open from my workspace, so I have not read any licence. Before you download a file, open its page and read the Licensing box.
- Safe to use: Public domain, CC0, or CC BY (credit the author in the description or end card).
- Be careful: CC BY-SA (it asks for share-alike), and anything marked "© Trustees of the British Museum" or "All rights reserved".
- A photo of a flat old document or painting is usually public domain. A photo of a 3D object (a stone, a coin, a cylinder) belongs to its photographer, so the page's licence matters.
- Send me the files named by item (`merneptah.jpg`, `tel-dan.jpg`, …) and I'll log the licence for each in `footage.md`.
