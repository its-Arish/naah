## Problems to settle

1. **Item 6 is the Temple Scroll, not the Great Isaiah Scroll.** The script line is "…prophets like Amos and Isaiah appear…". Either find the Great Isaiah Scroll file (`The_Great_Isaiah_Scroll_MS_A_(1QIsa)_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg`) or keep this one and change the label and line. A label saying "Isaiah" on this image would be wrong.
2. **Item 9** shows fallen stones, not the wall people know. If the line is "It's the Western Wall.", the picture should show the wall itself. This photo fits a different line: for example, "…stones thrown down from the Temple Mount…". I suggest keeping it for that line and sourcing a wall photo for the "Western Wall" line.
3. **Item 12** is a decorated copy, so the line and label say "Mishneh Torah" and not "his own handwriting".
4. **Item 20** stands in for Ben-Yehuda. If the script says "Ben-Yehuda revived Hebrew", the label must say "Committee of the Hebrew Language" and not name him.
5. **Item 11** shows a Greek-Hebrew psalm palimpsest. If the script line is about Jews of the medieval Middle East, say the Geniza is "a Cairo storeroom of about 1,000 years of Jewish writing" and let the label describe this fragment as it is.
