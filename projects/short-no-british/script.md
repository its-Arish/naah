# What If the British Never Came? (Short)

What if British never touch India? The honest answer is not the fairy tale you were expecting. In 1700, India made a quarter of everything produced on Earth. By 1950, around 4%. That's the part everybody knows. Here's the part people skip. Britain didn't defeat a strong, united India. In 1757, Clive had 3,000 men against 50,000. He won by buying the enemy commander. India, in 1750, was already cracked open. Nadir Shah had just robbed Delhi. The Marathas had just been crushed at Panipat. Dozens of kingdoms were hungry for money and weapons. So no, Britain does not automatically mean a rich, free India. It might mean someone else walks through that unlocked door. But Bengal would not have lost a third of its people in 1770, while tax collectors kept collecting, and Indian cloth would not have priced out by factories running on Indian cotton. So the real question is whether India would have been richer. It's whether it would even be one country, because the India we know was forged in resistance to the Raj.
