# How Things Fall

## Intro {nocard}
Every motion in this studio is computed from real physics, frame by frame.

## Act 1: Gravity
[On-screen: 1638 | Galileo]
Drop three crates. Same gravity, different bounciness.
A cannonball follows a perfect parabola, then bounces and rolls to a stop.

## Act 2: Things that swing
A tall pole topples like a real rigid rod. Slow at first, then fast.
A hanging sign swings and slowly settles.
Springs: bouncy, balanced, and stiff.

## Act 3: Life
And characters walk, wave and lean, with loose cloth that keeps moving after they stop.
