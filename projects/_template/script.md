# The Story of Tea

## Cold Open {nocard}
In 1610, a Dutch ship unloaded a strange dried leaf in Amsterdam.
Within two hundred years, an entire empire would be rearranged around it.

## Act 1: A Leaf from China
[On-screen: 2737 BC | Legend]
Legend says a Chinese emperor was boiling water when a few leaves blew into his pot.
He tasted it, liked it, and *tea* was born. Probably not true, but a great story.

[SKETCH]
A: Your Majesty, there are leaves in your water.
B: Leave them. I'm inventing something.
[/SKETCH]

## Act 2: Britain Gets Thirsty
[On-screen: 1662]
When a Portuguese princess married the English king, she brought tea with her.
Soon, the whole country was *hooked*.
